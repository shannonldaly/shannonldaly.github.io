An end-to-end job-search machine

Senior PM searches are highly targeted. You're watching specific companies for the right role to open, and applications do best when they land within 24 hours of the posting. Watching every job board and company site by hand, every day, is a job in itself, so I made the machine do the watching.

The pipeline

discover→ monitor→ score→ draft

Discovery

A Perplexity-powered research agent finds target companies against seven structured criteria: funding stage, size, remote policy, AI depth, tech stack, and relevance. Output is a structured company profile, not a link dump.

Monitoring

Target companies are watched across ATS platforms with state kept between runs, so new postings and removed roles surface as changes, not noise. An n8n and Gmail workflow handles the alerting. This replaced 2+ hours a day of manual checking.

The rating agent

Every new posting gets scored 1 to 10 by Claude against my written career criteria, with the rationale written back next to the score, so the sheet reads like a colleague triaged it and shows their work.

Fit briefs, not cover letters

Anything scoring 7 or higher gets a drafted writeup of how my experience maps to that specific role. I don't send these. Recruiters don't like AI-generated cover letters, and neither do I. The draft is a briefing for me: it shows me the angle, and then I write my own words.

How the scoring thinks

The rubric is written down, which is the whole point. A 9 means a direct domain match plus sweet-spot factors. A 7 means strong transferable experience and good company signals. Low scores carry their reasons too, so a pass is as legible as a pursue. Same principle as everything else on this site: judgment gets encoded, the machine applies it consistently, and the human reviews the calls.
